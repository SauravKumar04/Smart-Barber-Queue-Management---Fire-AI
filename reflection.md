# Reflection — QueueLess

## What I Got Wrong at the Start

Initially, I thought the main problem was that customers had to wait outside the salon because of limited space. But after talking to the barber, I found out that he loses around 10–15 customers on busy Sundays because they don't want to wait. This made me realize that the problem was not just about waiting time but also about losing customers and business.

## What I Would Simplify

I managed to collect only three real customer observations, which was a limitation of my project. I should have collected at least 20 entries and tested the system with more customers to check how accurately it estimates waiting time. Next time, I would spend more time collecting real data and testing the application in actual situations.

## Where AI Helped

AI helped me throughout the development process, from finding useful resources and understanding the problem to planning the complete flow of the application. It also helped me build the frontend and backend, connect the database, implement email notifications, and test the features. Whenever I faced errors or got stuck, AI helped me understand the issue and find a solution.

## Where AI Wasted Time

AI wasted a little time while I was troubleshooting the SMTP email notification issue. Some initial suggestions didn't work as expected, so I had to try different fixes. However, I was able to understand and solve the problem with AI's help. Overall, AI saved me much more time than it wasted.
