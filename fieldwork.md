# Fieldwork — Smart-Barber-Queue-Management

**Date:** 17 November 2026  
**Place:** Rupali Salon Masjid Chowk Hajipur Bihar 
**Person spoken to:** Barber/shop worker (Ravish Kumar Thakur)  
**Visit duration:** 3:10 - 3:50 PM

## What I saw

I visited the barber shop where I regularly get my hair cut. The shop is small, with limited space for customers to wait inside. Two people were at the shop when I started observing, and one more arrived while I was speaking to the barber. I took photos of the outside and inside of the shop.

## Conversation with the barber

These are my notes from the conversation, not direct quotes:

1. The shop has limited waiting space, so customers sometimes have to stand outside.
2. On busy days, customers may have to wait a long time even for a short service such as shaving. Some leave and go to another salon.
3. The barber said that two people handle the salon, and on busy Sundays he estimates losing around **10–15 customers** because of the rush. The shop is at a busy chowk (intersection).

## What surprised me

I have faced the waiting problem myself, so the general issue did not surprise me. What stood out was the barber's estimate that the shop can lose around 10–15 customers on busy Sundays. This is the barber's estimate, not a count I personally verified.

## How I collected the data

I recorded customer times in my phone notes while visiting the shop on 17 November 2026. I wrote down the arrival, service start, completion and service type for two customers. I also started an entry for a third customer, but did not record their times. The notes are saved in `data.csv`. Customer 2's recorded completion time is inconsistent with their start time, so I kept the original value and flagged it for verification rather than guessing a correction.

## Initial idea

A customer could scan a QR code to join a virtual queue and check their place without standing outside. When the barber finishes a service, the system would update the queue and email the next customer so they can return to the shop.
